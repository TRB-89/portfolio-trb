01 — about

Not someone who knows the tools. Someone who builds with them.

I'm Tarun, a developer and creative technologist working at the intersection of AI, software, and design. Rather than treating chatbots, code, and visuals as separate skills, I combine them into finished products — an assistant that actually ships, an interface that actually converts.

My day-to-day spans building AI chatbots and assistants, developing full applications, designing UI/UX in Figma, producing visual and brand work in Adobe Illustrator, and applying foundational machine learning — clustering and prediction — where it adds real value to a product.
